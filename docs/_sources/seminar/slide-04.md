# Crystal Eye science goals

Crystal Eye is a proposed space-based monitor designed to observe photons from roughly **10 keV to 30 MeV** across a wide field of view.

## Science goals and alerting

~~~{figure} ../figures/seminar/figure-04-1.webp
:alt: Source localization, field of view and rapid alert distribution diagram
:figclass: ce-seminar-figure

Source localization, field of view and rapid alert distribution diagram.
~~~

The main targets are short-duration transients for multimessenger studies and long gamma-ray bursts. The instrument concept combines a wide field of view, a response designed to vary relatively little with direction, and onboard localization. When a burst is found, the onboard system is designed to issue a prompt alert autonomously. An accurate position would help other observatories follow up while the burst or its counterpart is still observable.

The same measurements would support studies of MeV spectra and how sources change over time. The seminar also lists Earth occultation for known sources such as X-ray binaries and active galactic nuclei, as well as fast radio bursts, space weather and terrestrial gamma-ray flashes (TGFs).
