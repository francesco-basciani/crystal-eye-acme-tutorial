The closing figure connects the observing goals with the alert workflow. Crystal Eye is intended to cover the MeV gap and study source polarization while estimating directions on board, sending rapid alerts and supporting automated follow-up. The slide gives localization of about **1°** and processing of order **1 ms** as figures for the concept.

## Further reading

Aloisio et al. describe the instrument concept and its planned capabilities in *Astroparticle Physics*, volume 174 (2026), article 103171. The QR code from the closing slide also points readers to further information.

[Crystal Eye: All sky MeV monitor with high precision real-time localization](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.astropartphys.2025.103171)
