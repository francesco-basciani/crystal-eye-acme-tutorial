# Crystal Eye

Crystal Eye is a proposed all-sky monitoring concept for wide-field observations of the MeV sky. In this hands-on session, you will see how the instrument concept informs the simulations and how the simulated detector response can be used to detect a gamma-ray burst and estimate where it came from.
